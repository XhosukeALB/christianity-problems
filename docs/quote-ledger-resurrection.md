## Ancient non-Christian witnesses

- **Tacitus**, *Annals* 15.44: cited for the crucifixion under Pilate, not quoted. Text checked at Perseus: <https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.02.0078%3Abook%3D15%3Achapter%3D44>.
- **Josephus**, *Antiquities* 18.63: cited only as a partly interpolated witness; the essay explicitly cautions that the passage has been shaped by later Christian hands. Text checked at Perseus: <https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=J.+AJ+18.63&_pc=1>.

## Secondary literature

No direct quotations from modern scholars are used. Scholarly positions are paraphrased and cited in the footnotes and the Sources section. Bibliographic details (author, title, publisher, year) were checked against the published editions; no page-level quotation is attributed to a source that was not inspected.

## Notes

- No unconfirmed quotation marks remain in the case file.
- Where the ESV wording was not suitable as a stand-alone sentence, the sentence was paraphrased instead of silently altered.
- The quotation from Galatians ends with a semicolon because that is the ESV punctuation at the end of verse 16; the following clause is not part of the quotation.
